PATTERN STITCH
Granny Pattern
Row 1: Ch 3 (counts as dc), turn, 2 dc in first
ch-1 sp, * (ch 1, 3 dc in next ch-1 sp) to next
corner ch-2 sp, ch 1, (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in corner
ch-2 sp; rep from * once more, (ch 1, 3 dc in
next ch-1 sp) to end of row working last 3 dc in
beg ch-sp.

Row 2: Ch 4 (counts as dc, ch 1), turn, * (3 dc
in next ch-1 sp, ch 1) to next corner ch-2 sp, (3
dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in corner ch-2 sp, ch 1; rep from *
once more, (3 dc in next ch-1 sp, ch 1) to end of
row, dc in top of beg ch-3.
Rep Rows 1 and 2 for Granny pattern.
NOTES
1. Top is worked in 4 pieces: Left Top Panel,
Right Top Panel, and 2 Sleeves. Each Top
Panel forms one front and half of back.
2. Top Panels are worked in Granny pattern,
beg at armhole edge and working towards
center and lower edge of body. Panels are
sewn together along back edges.
3. Sleeves beg with a Granny Square. Granny
Squares are worked in joined rnds with
RS always facing you. Do not turn at beg
of rnds. Rows of Granny pattern are then
worked along some sides of the Square to
shape Sleeve.
4. Shorts are worked in 2 Panels. Panels are
worked in a simple granny pattern from the
lower edge upwards.
5. Yarn color is changed following Color
Sequences. To change yarn color when
working in rnds, fasten off old color at end
of rnd and join new color in any corner chsp. To change yarn color when working in
rows, work last st of old color to last yarn
over. Yarn over with new color and draw
through all loops on hook to complete the
st. Proceed with new color. Fasten off old
color.

Work 2 rows with A, 3 rows with B, 3 rows with
C, 3 rows with B, 3 rows with A, 3 rows with D,
3 rows with B, and 2 rows with A for a total of
22 rows in Top Color Sequence.

With A, ch 92.

Row 22: Work Row 1 of Granny pattern to last

Fasten off.
SLEEVES (make 2)
Granny Square
With D, ch 4; join with sl st in first ch to form a
ring.
Rnd 1 (RS): Ch 3 (counts as dc), 2 dc in ring,
(ch 2, 3 dc in ring) 3 times, ch 2; join with sl
st in top of beg ch-3 – you will have four 3-dc
groups and 4 ch-2 sps in this rnd.
Rnd 2: Sl st in each st to first ch-2 sp, sl st in
ch-2 sp, ch 3 (counts as dc), (2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in
same ch-2 sp (first corner made), * ch 1, (3 dc,
ch 2, 3 dc) in next ch-2 sp; rep from * 2 more
times, ch 1; join with sl st in top of beg ch-3 –
Eight 3-dc groups, 4 ch-1 sps, and 4 corner ch-2
sps (Two 3-dc groups along each of 4 sides
between corner ch-2 sps).

Work Granny Pattern along Three Sides
Granny pattern is now worked along only 3
sides of Granny Square.
Row 1 (RS): With B, sl st in each st to first ch-2
sp, sl st in ch-2 sp, ch 3 (counts as dc), 2 dc in
same ch-2 sp, * (ch 1, 3 dc in next ch-1 sp) to
next corner ch-2 sp, (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in ch-2
sp; rep from * once more, (ch 1, 3 dc in next
ch-1 sp) to next corner ch-2 sp, 3 dc in ch-2

sp and change to C in last dc; leave rem sts
unworked – Twenty-seven 3-dc groups, 24 ch-1
sps and 2 corner ch-2 sps.
Rows 2-6: Beg with Row 2 of pattern, work in
Granny pattern for 5 rows, working next 3 rows
with C and then 2 rows with B.
Do not fasten off B.
Work Granny Pattern along One Side
Row 7 (RS): With B, ch 3 (counts a dc), turn, 2
dc in first ch-1 sp, (ch 1, 3 dc) in next ch-1 sp) to
next corner ch-2 sp, ch 1, 3 dc in ch-2 sp; leave
rem sts unworked.
Row 8: With B, ch 4 (counts as dc, ch 1), (3 dc
in next ch-1 sp, ch 1) to end of row, dc in 3rd ch
of beg ch-4.
Fasten off.
From RS, draw up a loop of B in unworked
corner ch-2 sp of Row 6, and rep Rows 7 and
8 to work Granny pattern along opposite side
edge.
Fasten off.
FINISHING TOP
Place Panels on a flat surface with RS facing
you and with sts of long center back edges
matching. With A, sew or sl st center back
edges together.
Sew shoulder seams.
Place markers on front and back side edges,
about 8 1/2 in. (21.5 cm) from shoulder. Sew
Sleeves between markers.
Sew Sleeve and rem of side edges together
(along first row of Panels).
Bands
Rnd 1 (RS): From RS, join A with sl st in top left
corner of neck so that you are ready to work
down left front edge.
Work 66 sc evenly spaced down left front edge,
3 sc in lower left corner, 155 sc along lower
edge, 3 sc in lower right corner, 66 sc evenly
spaced up right front edge, ch 1 and work 79 sc
evenly spaced along neck edge; join with sl st
in first sc.

Row 2 (WS): Ch 1, turn, sl st in each neck edge
st to ch-1 sp, sc in ch-1 sp and in each sc along,
working 3 sc in center sc of each 3-sc corner;
do not join.
Work now proceeds in rows over front and

Collar
Place markers on neck edge, about 2 in. (5 cm)
from front corners.
Row 1 (RS): From RS, join A with sc at first
marker, sc in each st to 2nd marker.
Rows 2-10: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across.
Row 11: Ch 1, turn, sl st in each st across.
Fasten off.
Weave in ends.
SHORTS
SHORTS COLOR SEQUENCE
Work 1 row with A, 3 rows with B, 3 rows with
D, 3 rows with A, 3 rows with B, 3 rows with C,
3 rows with D, 3 rows with B, and 2 rows with C
for a total of 24 rows in Shorts Color Sequence.
SHORTS PANELS (make 2)
With A, ch 84.
Row 1 (RS): With A, work 2 dc in 4th ch from
hook (3 skipped ch count as dc), * ch 1, sk
next 3 ch, 3 dc in next ch; rep from * across
and change to B in last dc – Twenty-one 3-dc
groups and 20 ch-1 sps.
Row 2: With B, ch 4 (counts as dc, ch 1), turn, *
3 dc in next ch-1 sp, ch 1; rep from * across, dc
in top of beg ch-3 – Twenty 3-dc groups and 21
ch-1 sps.
Row 3 (Increase Row – RS): With B, ch 4
(counts as dc, ch 1), turn, 3 dc in first ch-1 sp,
ch 1, * 3 dc in next ch-1 sp, ch; rep from * to beg
ch-sp, (3 dc, ch 1, dc) in beg ch-sp – Twenty-

one 3-dc groups and 22 ch-1 sps.
Row 4: With B, ch 3 (counts as dc), turn, 2 dc
in first ch-1 sp, ch 1, * 3 dc in next ch-1 sp, ch 1;
rep from * to last ch-1 sp, 3 dc in last ch-1 sp
and change to D in last dc – Twenty-two 3-dc
groups and 21 ch-1 sps.
Row 5: With D, rep Row 2 – Twenty-one 3-dc
groups and 22 ch-1 sps.
Row 6: With D, rep Row 4 – Twenty-two 3-dc
groups and 21 ch-1 sps.
Shape Gusset
Row 7 (Decrease Row): With D, ch 1, turn, sl
st in each st to first ch-1 sp, sl st in ch-1 sp, ch
4 (counts as dc, ch 1), * 3 dc in next ch-1 sp,
ch 1; rep from * to last two 3-dc groups, dc
in last ch-1 sp and change to A; leave rem sts
unworked – Nineteen 3-dc groups and 20 ch-1
sps.
Continue to change yarn color following Shorts
Color Sequence until piece is complete.
Rows 8 and 9: Rep Rows 4 and 5 – Nineteen
3-dc groups and 20 ch-1 sps in Row 9.
Row 10 (Decrease Row): Ch 4 (counts as dc, ch
1), turn, sk first ch-1 sp, * 3 dc in next ch-1 sp, ch
1; rep from * to beg ch-sp, dc in 3rd ch of beg
ch-4 – Eighteen 3-dc groups and 19 ch-1 sps.
Rows 11-14: Rep Rows 4 and 5 twice.
Row 15: Rep Row 10 – Seventeen 3-dc groups
and 18 ch-1 sps.
Rows 16-21: Rep Rows 4 and 5 for 3 more
times.
Row 22: Rep Row 10 – Sixteen 3-dc groups and
17 ch-1 sps.
Rows 23 and 24: Rep Rows 4 and 5.
Fasten off.
Lower Edging

end of foundation ch – Twenty 3-dc groups and
21 ch-1 sps.
Row 2: Ch 1, turn, work sc evenly spaced across.
Rows 3 and 4: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across

FINISHING SHORTS
Fold each Panel in half lengthwise and sew
side edges together from lower edge to beg of
gusset shaping.
Sew Panels together from top edge down to
end of gusset shaping (at center of crotch).
Waistband
Row 1 (RS): From RS, join C with sl st in top
edge at front seam, work sc evenly spaced
around waist edge; do not join, work back and
forth in rows.
Rows 2-4: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across.
Row 5 (Turning Row): Ch 1, turn, working in
back loops only, sc in each st across.
Rows 6-8: Ch 1, turn, sc in each st across.
Fasten off.
Fold waistband in half along Turning Row and
sew top edge to inside of first row, leaving
edges at center front open for cord.

Cord
With A, make a chain about 56 in. (76 cm) long.
Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch
across.
Fasten off.
Attach a safety pin to one end of the cord then
use the safety pin to draw the cord through the
waistband.
Weave in ends.
